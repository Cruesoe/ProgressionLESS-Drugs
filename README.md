# ProgressionLESS: Drugs

**All the Progression goodness, less research.**

Research and overlap overhaul for drugs, so each drug unlocks in its proper tech era.

The "LESS" is less research: Progression: Drugs adds up to 73 research projects depending on your mods. This fork groups them into at most 13 themed projects, such as Smokeables, Alchemical remedies, Hard drugs, Herbal infusions, Vaccines and Addiction treatment, and moves the Vanilla Factions Expanded - Medieval 2 draughts to that mod's own alchemy research. Everything stays in the same era and at the same stage within it, and still needs the other research it did before.

Forked from [Progression: Drugs](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763005969) by ferny. Huge thanks to ferny for the original mod and all the Progression work this builds on.
